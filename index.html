Project 01 · Financial infrastructure

Offline UPI
Payment System

A prototype exploring how devices can exchange and settle UPI transactions over a simulated Bluetooth-style mesh network, without a live connection to a central backend.

  • Hybrid RSA-2048 and AES-256-GCM encryption for secure transaction relaying.
  • Atomic compare-and-set idempotency layer designed for exactly-once settlement.
  • Lamport clocks and a gossip protocol to detect potential double-spending.
